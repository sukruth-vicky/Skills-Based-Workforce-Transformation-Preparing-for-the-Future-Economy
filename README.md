🚀 Skills-Based Workforce Transformation | Case Study Analysis
📌 Overview

This project analyzes how a government agency redesigned its workforce strategy to adapt to a rapidly evolving, skills-driven economy. The focus is on transitioning from traditional degree-based hiring to a skills-first approach using data and technology.

🎯 Key Highlights
Shift from degree-based to skills-based hiring
Workforce analytics and labor market intelligence
Reskilling and upskilling strategies
Digital platforms for job matching and career guidance

🧠 Skills Demonstrated
Data Analysis
Business Intelligence
Workforce Analytics
Strategic Thinking
Problem Solving
